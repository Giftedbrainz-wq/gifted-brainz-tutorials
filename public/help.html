🔑 Forgot your password?

Passwords are stored securely, so nobody — not even an admin — can read yours. What an admin can do is set a brand new password on your account, hand it to you, and you change it later from My Account.

Here is exactly what to do:

  • Tap one of the WhatsApp numbers above (Support Line 1 handles login and account issues).
  • Send this: “Please reset my password. My name is ____ and my username is ____.”
  • An admin verifies that the account is yours, sets a temporary password and sends it to you.
  • Log in with the temporary password, then open My Account → Change password and set your own.
🔒 Never share your password with another student. Admins will only ever send you a new password — they will never ask you for your old one.

Still waiting for your account to be approved? New accounts are checked by an admin first. Go back to the login page and use Check my registration status to see where yours has got to.

Quick fixes before you message us

  • “Server not found” when logging in. The portal wakes up its server on the first request. Wait a few seconds and press Login again — the app now retries automatically.
  • A page looks out of date. Pull down to refresh, or close and reopen the app; updates install quietly in the background.
  • A file will not open. Use Download instead of View in app — some file types need your device's own reader.
  • Forgotten password. See Forgot your password? above — message us with your username and an admin resets it for you.
  • “Waiting for admin approval”. A brand new account needs an admin to approve it. Check the status from the login page; it usually takes a few hours.
